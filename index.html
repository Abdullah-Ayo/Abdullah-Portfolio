06 // Retail Merchandising & Margin Strategy

Delmart: Volume vs. Margin Drivers

Retail analytics dashboard for cycling merchant Delmart Retail Ltd dissecting 113,223 transactions and ₦95.22M in sales across 17 products.

ChallengeLeadership was allocating marketing budget equally across high-volume items without understanding which product lines were actually generating gross margins.

SolutionDecoupled the volume driver from the margin driver: proved Bikes generated 72.69% of revenue (₦69.22M) on only 14.9% of unit sales, while Clothing generated 62.1% of units but fractional revenue.

Key FindingUncovered a measure mislabeling error in the existing dashboard where total order quantities were accidentally reported under sales revenue titles.

Complete database transformation pipeline cleaning, deduplicating, and standardizing a raw global layoffs dataset in MySQL for downstream analytics.
